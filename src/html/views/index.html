Customs & Certification

Certified customs operations with infrastructure for high-value and regulated goods.

  • Temporary & permanent customs warehouse
  • 2 customs inspection zones
  • Customs guarantee allowing the acceptance of goods at a customs warehouse worth up to 1 million euros
  • BRC Storage & Distribution certified
  • ISO 9001 certified
The Trans Tok warehouse has two customs inspection zones for goods:
  • – an on-site vehicle clearance zone without check-in at Polish customs terminals
  • – an area with the customs status of a temporary storage warehouse and a dedicated permanent customs storage warehouse, which allows receiving and consolidating goods, sorting and labeling without customs clearance
